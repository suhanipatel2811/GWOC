Morning Grounding

Start your day with stability and clear intention. This guided practice helps you connect with the present moment and set a calm, focused tone for the day ahead.

schedule 10 min listen self_improvement Guided Practice

What is Grounding?

Grounding is a mindfulness practice that brings your awareness to the present moment by connecting you with your physical body and immediate surroundings. It's especially powerful in the morning when your mind might be racing with thoughts about the day ahead.

This practice helps anchor you in the "now," reducing anxiety about the future and creating a stable foundation for your day. Regular morning grounding can improve focus, emotional regulation, and overall well-being.

The Practice: 10-Minute Guide

1

Find Your Space (1 min)

Sit comfortably with your feet flat on the floor. You can be on a chair, cushion, or bed. Close your eyes or soften your gaze downward.

2

Body Scan (3 min)

Bring awareness to your body. Notice the sensation of your feet on the ground, your back against the chair, the weight of your hands. Don't judge—just observe.

3

Breath Awareness (3 min)

Follow your natural breath. Notice the cool air entering your nose, the rise and fall of your chest. If your mind wanders, gently return to the breath.

4

Set Your Intention (2 min)

Silently affirm: "I am present. I am grounded. I am ready." Choose one word for your day (peace, courage, joy) and carry it with you.

5

Gentle Return (1 min)

Slowly wiggle your fingers and toes. Take a deep breath. When ready, open your eyes and begin your day with presence.

Tips for Success

alarm

Same Time Daily

Practice at the same time each morning to build consistency. Even 5 minutes is valuable.

phone_disabled

No Devices

Avoid checking your phone before practicing. Give yourself this time before engaging with external demands.

favorite

Be Gentle

Some mornings will feel easier than others. That's normal. The practice is showing up, not perfection.
